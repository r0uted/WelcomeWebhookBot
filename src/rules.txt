<b>📘 Community Rules & Expectations</b>

Consent is mandatory — Every interaction must be clearly consensual. No pressure, no assumptions, no coercion of any kind.
Respect personal boundaries — If someone declines, changes their mind, or seems unsure, the conversation stops immediately.
Privacy is essential — Do not share names, photos, conversations, or personal details outside the group.
No explicit content in public spaces — Keep discussions appropriate for a general audience. Private conversations should follow consent and comfort guidelines.
Be kind and mature — Treat everyone with courtesy. Harassment, insults, or disrespectful behavior are not tolerated.
No discrimination — This community welcomes adults of all backgrounds. Racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, or any form of prejudice is prohibited.
No illegal activity — All members must follow local laws and platform rules.
No solicitation or commercial activity — This is a community space, not a marketplace.
Communicate clearly — Be honest about your intentions, comfort levels, and expectations. Healthy communication keeps everyone safe.
Admins have final say — Moderators may remove posts or members who violate rules to protect the community.
